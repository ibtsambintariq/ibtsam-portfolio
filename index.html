About Me

I build real, working websites — not mockups. Front-end is my core: clean code, modern layouts, and sites that actually work for agencies and brands.

With 1.5 years of hands-on web development — started in 9th class — plus 7 months of GHL and 7-8 months of Meta Ads, all office-based in 3 offices. I build real, working websites for agencies and brands.
